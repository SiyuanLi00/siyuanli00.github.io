<div class="intro">
<h1>Hi, I am Siyuan Li (李思源)</h1>
<p>I am a <b>Ph.D. candidate</b> with the <a href="https://www.cs.sjtu.edu.cn/">School of Computer Science</a> at <a href="https://www.sjtu.edu.cn/">Shanghai Jiao Tong University</a>, advised by <a href="https://www.cs.sjtu.edu.cn/jiaoshiml/lijianhua.html">Prof. Jianhua Li</a>, and a <b>joint Ph.D. student</b> with the <a href="https://www.ntu.edu.sg/computing/">College of Computing and Data Science</a> at <a href="https://www.ntu.edu.sg/">Nanyang Technological University</a>, advised by <a href="https://dr.ntu.edu.sg/entities/person/Tao-Dacheng">Prof. Dacheng Tao</a>. I received my <b>B.Eng. in Information Security</b> from the <a href="https://www.cs.sjtu.edu.cn/">School of Computer Science</a>, Shanghai Jiao Tong University in 2022, advised by <a href="https://infosec.sjtu.edu.cn/DirectoryDetail.aspx?id=125">Prof. Jun Wu</a>.</p>
<p>My research focuses on <b>trustworthy foundation model and agents</b>, especially the <b>attack and defense of AI agent systems</b>, <b>content security of LLMs</b>, along with the <b>risks of the agent application in realistic or industrial systems</b>. I have published <b>more than 10 papers</b> in top venues including <b>AAAI</b>, <b>ACL</b>, <b>RTSS</b>, <b>TSC</b>, and <b>TIFS</b>, and serve as a reviewer for <b>ICML</b>, <b>ICLR</b>, <b>AAAI</b>, <b>MM</b>, <b>TDSC</b>, <b>TIFS</b>, <b>TSC</b>, and <b>TMC</b>. I am the recipient of the <b>Ant Group Ph.D. Innovation Grant</b> (¥60,000).</p>
</div>
