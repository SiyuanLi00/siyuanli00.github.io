<div class="intro">
<h1>Hi, I am Siyuan Li</h1>
<p>I am a <b>Ph.D. candidate</b> in the <a href="https://www.cs.sjtu.edu.cn/">School of Computer Science</a> at <a href="https://www.sjtu.edu.cn/">Shanghai Jiao Tong University</a>, advised by <b>Prof. Jianhua Li</b>, and currently a <b>joint Ph.D. student</b> between <a href="https://www.ntu.edu.sg/">Nanyang Technological University</a> and Shanghai Jiao Tong University. I received my <b>B.Eng. in Information Security</b> from Shanghai Jiao Tong University in 2022, advised by <a href="https://infosec.sjtu.edu.cn/DirectoryDetail.aspx?id=125">Prof. Jun Wu</a>.</p>
<p>My research focuses on <b>AI security</b> and <b>trustworthy AI</b>, specifically the <b>reliability and safety of AI agents</b>, <b>attack and defense in multi-agent systems</b>, and <b>content security of large language models (LLMs)</b>, along with security risks of generative AI and intelligent edge/IoT systems. I have published <b>10+ papers</b> in top venues including <b>AAAI</b>, <b>IEEE TSC</b>, <b>IEEE TIFS</b>, and <b>IEEE Communications Magazine</b>, and serve as a reviewer for <b>ICML</b>, <b>ICLR</b>, and <b>AAAI</b>. I am the recipient of the <b>Ant Group Ph.D. Innovation Grant</b> (¥60,000, as student PI) and the <b>COSCO Shipping Scholarship</b> (top 5%).</p>
<p>I organize my research around the following framework, spanning security across the AI stack:</p>
