**Siyuan Li** is a Ph.D. candidate in the School of Computer Science at Shanghai Jiao Tong University, advised by Prof. Jianhua Li. He received his B.Eng. in Information Security from Shanghai Jiao Tong University in 2022, and continued his graduate studies there, transitioning to the Ph.D. program in April 2024.

His research lies in **trustworthy AI** and **multi-agent systems**, with a focus on security risks of generative AI and intelligent edge/IoT systems. Specifically, he works on:

- **Multi-agent Systems**: Security risks of LLM-driven agents and multi-agent reinforcement learning; real-world applications in content security risk control and industrial IoT.
- **Trustworthy Generative AI**: Security abuses of LLM / text-to-image models (e.g., jailbreaking, agent misuse); detection mechanisms for AI-generated content.

He has published over 10 papers at top venues including AAAI, IEEE TSC, IEEE TIFS, and IEEE Communications Magazine, and serves as a reviewer for ICML, ICLR, AAAI, and multiple IEEE journals.

## Projects & Funding

**Leading:**

- **Privacy-Preserving Tabular Data Synthesis with Multi-Agent Systems** — Ant Group Sponsored Project, Sep. 2025 – May 2026
  - PI of the Ph.D. Innovation Grant (60,000 RMB), supported by Ant Group & Cyberspace Security College.
  - Addressed the challenge of sensitive tabular-data sharing: vanilla differential-private synthesis suffers semantic inconsistency and lacks practical local-remote split schemes.
  - Built an AutoGen-powered multi-agent closed-loop pipeline: generation under DP constraints, privacy auditing, and utility evaluation. The system filters and repairs abnormal samples to balance privacy protection and data quality.
